**Module working title:** After Acts: The End of Paul

**Audience need:** Acts ends abruptly in Rome with no martyrdom recorded. Visitors ask what happened next.

**Tone:** Distinguish Scripture from tradition. Present extra-biblical paths as **forks with sources and confidence**, not settled fact.

---

## Goal

A concluding module that (1) states clearly what Scripture says and does not say, then (2) offers branching traditions readers can inspect without the site pretending the question is closed.

---

## What Scripture says / doesn’t

| Source | What it gives | What it does not settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acts 28 | Paul in Rome, under guard, proclaiming; narrative stops open | No martyrdom scene; no Spain trip narrated |
| 2 Timothy 4:6–8 | Farewell tone; “poured out,” crown of righteousness — *if* read as late Pauline | Implies approaching death / execution in many readings; genre and dating still marked Consensus vs Wider where the site already does |

Authoring rule: never present Nero, Spain, or a beheading as “what the Bible says” unless the text actually says it.

## Extra-scriptural forks (outline)

Present each with **source** and **confidence level** (e.g. early tradition / later legend / archaeological memory). Forks are options to explore, not a forced single ending.

### 1. Rome and the sword

- **1 Clement 5** (~96 AD): Paul martyred; language of reaching the “furthest limits of the west” (interpretive debate: Spain vs. rhetorical west).
- **Acts of Paul** (2nd cent.): Beheaded under Nero; famous “milk instead of blood” motif — treat as legendary narrative, low confidence as history.
- Later church memory: martyrdom in Rome under Nero as the dominant traditional ending.

### 2. The Spanish question

- **Romans 15:24, 28** — Paul’s stated plan to go to Spain via Rome.
- Acts silent on fulfillment.
- Later writers and the “furthest west” line in 1 Clement as disputed evidence.
- Site framing: **plan attested; fulfillment contested** — Consensus vs Wider notes only where the project already distinguishes dating/fulfillment elsewhere (e.g. Spain on the timeline).

### 3. Relics and memory

- **Basilica of Saint Paul Outside the Walls** on the Via Ostiense.
- Feast day **June 29** (shared traditional memory with Peter in Rome).
- Role: how the church remembered Paul — not a substitute for Acts.
