**Module working title:** Before Paul: The Persecutor

**Audience need:** Visitors who wonder why Paul persecuted followers of “the Way.”

**Tone:** An honest look at religious conviction turned to violence — not hagiography.

---

## Goal

Give a short, expandable pre-conversion entry that explains *why* Saul of Tarsus opposed the early Jesus movement, then link forward into Paul’s own later references to that past.

---

## Outline (narrative spine)

1. **Pharisee credentials** — Training and identity under Gamaliel; zeal for the ancestral traditions.
2. **Theological scandal** — A crucified messiah as cursed under the law (Deut 21:23); why “the Way” was not a minor disagreement.
3. **Flashpoint: Stephen** — Martyrdom and the outbreak of persecution (Acts 7–8).
4. **Turning point: Damascus road** — Acts 9 as the hinge from persecutor to apostle.
5. **Paul’s own later voice** — How he names that past in the letters (not a scrubbed biography).
